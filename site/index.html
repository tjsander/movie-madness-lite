An unaffiliated fan project, not endorsed by or connected with Movie Madness. Catalog listings are the property of Movie Madness; being listed doesn't mean a copy is on the shelf today. Data from movie-madness-pdx-tmdb.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Searching for an actor or director finds only the titles on their named shelves (like "KUROSAWA, AKIRA"), not every film they're in. To see which of their films Movie Madness has, browse them on Letterboxd with the mmboxmonkey script, which adds Movie Madness availability to Letterboxd film pages.
